# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SvaraFlow to be a place where anyone can contribute regardless of
experience level, background, identity or language. We pledge to make
participation a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give technical feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change; ask why instead of relitigating.
- Keep discussion focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any project chat. It also applies when representing the
project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a GitHub issue that asks for a private channel, or
by contacting the maintainer listed in `Cargo.toml` (`Th3 A6add0n`) through
GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily
ban, or permanently ban a participant, and will explain the decision to the
reporter.
